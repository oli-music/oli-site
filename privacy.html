Privacy Policy

Oli ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our applications (the "App"), including the Android app, desktop app, and this website's web player.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account information — if you connect a third-party service (e.g. Spotify) for playlist import, we may access your username and basic profile information.
  • Usage data — songs played, paused, and skipped; playlists created and modified; search queries; app settings and preferences.

Device Information

  • Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • Crash reports and performance metrics, if you've opted into analytics
  • IP address and network type, for basic connectivity and analytics purposes

Music Data

  • Access to your device's local music files, for local playback
  • Streaming metadata from YouTube Music and any other connected services
  • Your created playlists and song collections

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide music streaming, playback, and library/playlist management
  • To sync your preferences across your own devices, where that feature is used
  • To power search, discovery, and recommendation features
  • To diagnose and fix bugs, if crash/performance reporting is enabled

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We integrate with third-party services (such as YouTube Music, and optionally Spotify) to provide streaming content — using Oli means your requests necessarily pass through those services under their own terms.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

Listen Together

If you use the real-time "Listen Together" feature, session data (track info, playback position, participant identifiers you choose to share) is relayed through our server infrastructure to synchronize playback between participants. This data is not persisted beyond what's needed to run the session.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

Since Oli is open source, you can inspect exactly what data the app collects and how it's handled by reading the source code directly. Analytics and crash reporting, where present, can typically be disabled in Settings.

Children's Privacy

Oli is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
