Questions

Before you ask

Is Oli really free?+

Yes — no subscription tiers, no premium unlock, no ads to skip. It's free because it's open source and community-run, not because your data is the product.

Where does the music come from?+

Oli streams from existing platforms rather than hosting content itself. Playback quality and catalog depend on what's available there.

Is there a desktop version?+

Yes — Windows, macOS, and Linux builds are available. Check the oli-desktop repo for the latest release.

Can I contribute?+

Absolutely — it's GPL-3.0 licensed and built in the open. Pull requests, issues, and forks are all welcome on GitHub.
